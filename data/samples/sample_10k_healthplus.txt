ITEM 1. BUSINESS
HealthPlus Therapeutics Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing novel immuno-oncology therapies and precision gene-editing treatments for rare metabolic diseases. Our commercial portfolio includes OncoTarget-4 and specialized metabolic enzyme replacements.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
Our business, financial condition, and results of operations are subject to numerous risks, including the following:
1. Clinical Trial Failures: Clinical development involves high risk and long timelines. If our Phase 3 oncology candidates fail to show statistically significant efficacy, commercialization will be halted.
2. FDA Regulatory Approvals: Delay or denial of Biologics License Applications (BLAs) from the U.S. FDA or EMA would severely impede revenue potential.
3. Patent Expirations & Biosimilar Competition: Expiration of key substance patents on early generation therapies may expose us to aggressive generic competition.
4. Supply Chain & Biomanufacturing Disruption: Dependency on single-source sterile contract manufacturing organizations (CMOs) for specialized biologics.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS (MD&A)
The following discussion highlights our financial performance for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 compared to 2024:
- Revenue: Commercial product sales and partnership licensing revenues reached $680.0M in FY25 vs $510.0M in FY24, representing strong 33.3% YoY expansion.
- Operating Expenses: Heavy Phase 3 R&D investment drove operating expenses to $490.0M in FY25 vs $395.0M in FY24.
- Net Income: GAAP Net Income surged to $145.0M in FY25 vs $82.0M in FY24, fueled by European commercial launch milestones.
- Diluted EPS: Diluted earnings per share was $2.15 vs $1.25 in FY24.
- Operating Cash Flow: Operating cash flow generated $210.0M in FY25 vs $130.0M in FY24, maintaining a healthy cash runway of over 36 months.

Management Guidance:
For FY26, management projects top-line product revenues between $800M and $850M, anticipating preliminary top-line readout for the Phase 3 rare disease trial in Q3 FY26.
