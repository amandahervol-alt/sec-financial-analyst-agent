ITEM 1. BUSINESS
RetailHub Global Inc. operates a global omnichannel consumer goods marketplace, automated fulfillment network, and subscription loyalty membership platform serving over 45 million active households across North America and Europe.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
Key operational and market risks facing our business include:
1. Consumer Spending Contraction & Inflation: Persistent macroeconomic inflation may reduce discretionary household spending across non-essential consumer categories.
2. Supply Chain & Freight Logistics Volatility: Global shipping lane congestion and fuel surcharges may compress gross merchandise margins.
3. Cybersecurity Threats: High volume of e-commerce customer transaction records creates constant vulnerability to cyber intrusions and payment gateway breaches.
4. Intense E-Commerce Competition: Price competition from multinational direct-to-consumer and retail mega-platforms.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS (MD&A)
Financial Results for FY25 compared to FY24:
- Revenue: Net marketplace revenue and fulfillment services generated $3,250.0M in FY25 vs $2,840.0M in FY24 (+14.4% YoY).
- Operating Expenses: Warehouse robotics automation and shipping efficiency kept operating expenses at $2,420.0M in FY25 vs $2,210.0M in FY24.
- Net Income: Net Income reached $460.0M in FY25 vs $340.0M in FY24 (+35.3% YoY).
- Diluted EPS: Diluted EPS reached $4.20 vs $3.15 in FY24.
- Operating Cash Flow: Operating Cash Flow rose to $680.0M in FY25 vs $520.0M in FY24.

Management Guidance:
Management forecasts FY26 net sales growth of 12% to 15%, fueled by automated micro-fulfillment centers and high-margin retail media advertising network expansion.
